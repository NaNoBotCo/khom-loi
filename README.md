# Khom Loi, Drawn · โคมลอย วาดด้วยคณิต

The Yi Peng sky lantern drawn with the physics of hot air: why it rises, one flight from lighting to landing, where the wind brings forty lanterns down over Chiang Mai, and how many are up at once. English and Thai.

Live: https://motdang.net/sites/khom-loi/ · https://nanobotco.github.io/khom-loi/

- `docs/lantern.js` — the lantern: ideal-gas lift, fuel burn, heat in and out, drag, wind growing with height.
- `docs/app.js` — every drawing on the page.
- `tools/copy_text.py` — every word, both languages; `tools/build.py` writes the pages (`--motdang` for the motdang.net copy).
- `tools/mapdata.py` — cuts water and the runway out of Mot Dang's map (OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL).
- `research/facts.md` — the sourced notes behind the page.
